\section{Accounting for the Eurosystem purchase experiment}
\label{app:eurosystem-purchases}

The financial calculation conditions on exposures and reconciled net resources. Household-saving optimality and a model of firms' portfolio choices are unnecessary for this conditional exercise. An optional household completion is provided in the main-paper appendix. Probability-weighted repayment and transfer statistics are computed after clearing; they do not determine positions.

\paragraph{Transaction and timing.}
Before the sovereign state is realized, the Eurosystem purchases a specified fraction of the four bank blocks' holdings of selected sovereign issuers, at the model's book value of one. Let $x_{uv}$ denote a claim from creditor node $u$ to debtor node $v$, let $B_i$ denote bank block $i$, $G_g$ sovereign $g$, and $E$ the Eurosystem. Write $\mathbf{P}=(P_{ig})$ for the purchase matrix, with $P_{ig}$ the purchase from bank block $i$ of sovereign $g$. In the reporting matrix, the transaction is
\[
 x_{B_i,G_g}'=x_{B_i,G_g}-P_{ig},\qquad
 x_{E,G_g}'=x_{E,G_g}+\sum_iP_{ig},\qquad
 x_{B_i,E}'=x_{B_i,E}+\sum_gP_{ig}.
\]
The first identity is bilateral; the second applies once to each issuer and the third once to each bank. Bank sales create an equal safe reserve claim on the Eurosystem, consistent with the balance-sheet mechanics described in \citet{ECBReserves2021}, Box 3. Government debt columns and bank asset totals are unchanged. Eurosystem assets and liabilities both increase by $\sum_{ig}P_{ig}$. The source matrix is preserved; counterfactual matrices are saved separately.

Let $\mathbf{b}=(b_i)$, $\mathbf{D}=(D_i)$ and $\mathbf{E}=(E_i)$ collect reference resources, clearing debts and accounting equity; $\mathbf1$ is the vector of ones. In the financial system, $\mathbf{N}'=\mathbf{N}-\mathbf{P}$ and $\mathbf{b}'=\mathbf{b}+\mathbf{P}\mathbf1$. All bank debt, accounting equity, headroom, distress cutoffs, interbank claims and private-loan impairments retain their baseline values. In particular,
\[
 \mathbf{b}'+\mathbf{B}\mathbf1+\mathbf{N}'\mathbf1=\mathbf{D}+\mathbf{E}.
\]
Risk-weighted capital requirements are not recomputed. The reserve claim repays one. Since the baseline sovereign repayment exceeds one in survival states under the pricing normalization, the exchange can reduce bank receipts in those states; the complete scenario grid retains this effect.

\paragraph{Risk transfer.}
Holding bank repayments fixed, the aggregate change in bank resources equals the Eurosystem's additional net cash shortfall:
\[
 \sum_i\Delta A_i(s)=\sum_{ig}P_{ig}[1-R_g^G(s)].
\]
This measure is negative in survival states when sovereign receipts exceed reserve payments. Its expectation under the existing pricing probabilities is zero by construction of $R_g^G$, not evidence of zero economic or fiscal cost. The reported maximum is a state-contingent cash exposure. The model does not allocate it among national central banks or feed it back to sovereign or household resources. Actual public-sector purchase arrangements distinguish national holdings and loss sharing \citep{ECBPSPPFAQ}; the aggregate node cannot identify those incidences. The experiment also abstracts from market-price effects, lending responses and purchases from nonbank sellers.

\paragraph{Clearing and checks.}
For each purchase matrix, all bank default sets are re-enumerated in each of the sixteen sovereign states. The unchanged NBFI buffer bound continues to imply full debt repayment. Independent eight-node bank--NBFI enumeration verifies all states for purchases of half of all issuer holdings, half of French holdings and three quarters of Italian and Spanish holdings. The zero-purchase case reproduces every baseline equilibrium. Accounting checks verify government debt stocks, bank asset totals, full equity and the equality of direct resource gains and Eurosystem cash exposure. Critical floor capacities are recomputed for the post-purchase network using the same proportional rule, with endpoint checks. The selected published capacities are attained minima; no optimization over purchase composition or quantity is claimed.

The R-only driver \texttt{main.R} reproduces the experiment, its publication table and validation. Saved results include state and bank outcomes, counterfactual matrices and validation records. The frozen calibration and underlying exposure register are not overwritten.
