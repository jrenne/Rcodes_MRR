\medskip
This appendix first establishes the repayment-floor capacity results. It then explains the design and construction of the public-data exposure network, separating source observations, bilateral allocations and accounting reconciliation from the assumptions governing distress. A final section documents the Eurosystem purchase experiment. The baseline is the end-2024 residence network with public OCR headroom, full accounting equity and bank $\lambda=.90$.
